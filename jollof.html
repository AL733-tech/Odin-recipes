Jollof Recipe

Description

Jollof rice is a vibrant orange red one pot dish celebrated for its deeply savory flavor, aromatic spice profile, and fluffy texture. It features grains of fragrant Jasmine or long grain perfumed rice that are completely stained by a rich sauce made from blended fresh tomatoes, red bell peppers, onions, and fiery scotch bonnet peppers. The dish carries a distinct, comforting warmth from a unique blend of local spices, including curry powder, ginger, garlic, nutmeg, and rosemary, all balanced by a savory backbone of meat stock and bouillon. Unlike other versions that can be smoky or heavy, the variety is prized for its separate, non sticky grains that lock in a bright, sweet and spicy tomato essence in every single bite.

Ingredients

  • Jasmine rice
  • Fresh tomatoes
  • Tomato paste
  • Onions
  • Nutmeg
  • Beef
  • Vegetable oil
  • Ginger
  • Garlic

Steps

  1. Blend the base: Puree the fresh tomatoes, red bell peppers, half the onions, and scotch bonnet peppers until smooth.
  2. Fry the onions and paste: Heat vegetable oil in a pot, sauté the remaining chopped onions, then add the tomato paste and fry for several minutes until it darkens.
  3. Simmer the sauce: Pour in the blended puree, garlic, ginger, curry powder, thyme, nutmeg, rosemary, and bouillon cubes, then simmer until the oil rises to the top.
  4. Toast the rice: Add the washed Jasmine rice directly into the sauce and stir for a few minutes so the grains absorb the flavor.
  5. Add stock and steam: Pour in the stock, stir, and bring to a simmer. Cover the pot tightly with foil and a lid, then cook on low heat until the rice is tender and dry.
